Country introduction

Welcome to Italy.

Italy is a country known for its ancient history, beautiful art, delicious food, and regional traditions.

About the country

Italy is located in southern Europe. It is shaped like a boot and is surrounded by the Mediterranean Sea.

Brief History

Italy was home to the Roman Empire and later became the center of the Renaissance. The country became unified in 1861.

What Italy is Known For

Italy is known for Roman landmarks, Renaissance art, fashion, architecture, music, and its strong local traditions.

Italian Food

Pizza, pasta, gelato, cheese, and regional recipes are important parts of everyday life in Italy.

Landscape

Italy has mountains, lakes, beaches, vineyards, countryside, and famous cities such as Rome, Florence, and Venice.
